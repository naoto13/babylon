# Game Screen Image Prompts

12枚とも組み込みのImage Genを使い、個別に生成した。生成時の共通条件と、各画面固有の最終指示を以下に記録する。

## 共通条件

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset: 16:9 desktop browser game gameplay screenshot, target `1440 x 810`
- Premium stylized 3D game art achievable in Babylon.js
- Actual gameplay frame, not key art or a title screen
- One game concept per image
- No browser chrome, device bezel, logo, or watermark
- HUD is sparse and uses symbols and digits where possible

## 01 Chrono Arena

Circular ruined clockwork arena suspended over a dark void. Show one agile hero fighting a dense but readable shadow swarm, with a boss telegraph and projectiles. Use a fixed high-angle three-quarter top-down camera at roughly 55 degrees, with the arena edge visible. Include a large `00:37` timer, health, three ability icons, cyan time fractures, warm brass, and deep navy lighting.

## 02 One-Minute Heist

High-security museum at night with cutaway walls, display cases, lasers, cameras, guards, a vault, and a valuable artifact. Show a masked thief and a translucent ghost from a previous run distracting a guard. Use an orthographic-like isometric camera at roughly 45 degrees, fixed per room. Include `00:42`, loot value, detection, and exit indicators.

## 03 Last Signal

Tiny spherical asteroid outpost with a central beacon, modular turrets, energy walls, minerals, and incoming alien drones. Show an engineer-soldier firing while placing a holographic turret. Use an elevated third-person camera behind the player, freely orbitable around the beacon, with the asteroid curvature visible. Include `00:18`, beacon shield, resources, and four build slots.

## 04 Moonlit Potion Workshop

Cozy alchemist workshop at night. Show first-person hands grinding luminous petals at a wooden workbench, beside a heated glass cauldron, ingredient trays, and an illustrated order parchment. The camera is fixed to the workbench with limited head-look. Use mostly diegetic UI: temperature arc, ingredient icons, and held-tool highlight.

## 05 Rune Mechanism Laboratory

Circular stone-and-brass research table in an arcane observatory. Show detection, repeat, direction, and fire runes connected into a circuit that launches a curving test fireball toward a wooden target. Use an oblique overhead camera at roughly 50 degrees, freely orbitable and zoomable. Include a sparse radial palette, energy meter, and test button.

## 06 Cursed Relic Restorer

Victorian occult restoration studio with rain-streaked windows and a protective salt circle. Center an ornate cracked music box levitating over an inspection stand; reveal hidden runes with a magical lens and show red curse energy escaping a crack. Use an object-centered inspection camera with 360-degree orbit and zoom. Include three tool icons, stability gauge, and damage markers.

## 07 Pocket Planet Delivery Company

Charming miniature green planet with mountains, ocean, forest, mine, factory, town, trains, and trucks. Show a railway being dragged around the globe with alternate routes previewed. Use a free-orbit globe camera showing roughly two-thirds of the sphere, making curvature unmistakable. Include a compact construction toolbar and delivery goals.

## 08 Orbital Chain

Compact fictional solar system with a warm star, four planets, moons, stations, and freighters. Show a planned transfer from a desert planet to an ocean moon, with a curved route and an arrival-window wedge. Use a macro strategic camera obliquely above the orbital plane with pan, zoom, and orbit. Include a thin time scrubber, speed controls, cargo counts, and fuel ring.

## 09 Living Planet

Continuous landscape with forested mountains, river delta, farms, town, factory, and freight roads. Preview a road cutting through a wildlife corridor and watershed, with an alternate tunnel route. Use a high oblique strategic camera around 60 degrees downward, freely rotatable and zoomable. Paint ecological overlays on the terrain and keep management UI compact.

## 10 Abyss Photographer

Bioluminescent deep-ocean canyon with coral chimneys and drifting particles. Show a rare translucent whale-like creature performing a feeding spiral while a predator silhouette approaches from darkness. Use a first-person free-swimming diver camera with a compact underwater camera in the foreground. Include viewfinder corners, focus, oxygen, light, and photo-quality indicators.

## 11 Wreck Salvage

Huge rusted ocean-liner wreck on the abyssal floor. Show a diver cutting a steel panel while tethering a heavy antique crate to two lift bags; the swinging crate, wreck opening, and nearby submersible must all be visible. Use a freely orbitable third-person over-the-shoulder camera. Include oxygen, tether tension, tool heat, and cargo mass.

## 12 Echo Diver

Near-total darkness in a vast underwater cave with rock arches and ancient ruins. Show a small diver moving left to right and emitting cyan sonar waves that temporarily reveal 3D surfaces; a distant magenta echo hints at a creature. Use a fixed side-on 2.5D camera with foreground, gameplay lane, and background parallax. Include only oxygen, sonar charge, and sound-risk indicators.
